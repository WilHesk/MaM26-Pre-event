The Concept

Rather than one static site, the mile is programmed end to end. A kids' activity at one end while something like a mental-health film screening runs at the other, pulling families, skaters, older and younger crowds through each other's spaces as they move along. Whether to run an organised mass move (as in Salford) is still undecided — Salford's numbers were small and easy to manage; 1,000+ people is a different logistical question.
