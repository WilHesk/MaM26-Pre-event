The Concept

A one-mile celebration of creativity, community and wellbeing is taking over Stockport on Sunday 27 September. After the sucess of the Salford event, GRIT Studios and The Coping partner again to launch Move a Mile Stockport – a free, family-friendly festival inviting people of all ages to explore a vibrant one-mile trail packed with creativity, culture and fun.

This event is free & open to all, register for a free Eventbrite ticket now.
